Principal Investigator

MG

Matthew S. Gentry, PhD

Professor & Chair

Matthew Gentry is a brain metabolism scientist whose work has shaped how the field understands brain glycogen and glucose metabolism, and what happens to the nervous system when those pathways break. He has worked on glycogen storage diseases for nearly twenty years.

After a PhD in molecular biology at Syracuse University (2003), he took up Lafora disease, a glycogen storage disease and childhood dementia, as a postdoctoral scholar with Jack Dixon at UC San Diego, where he defined the biochemical properties of the genes mutated in the disease. That work has been continuously funded by the NIH since 2007: a K99/R00, multiple R01s, a P01, and a recent R35 on Brain Glycogen: Metabolism, Mechanisms, and Therapeutic Potential, alongside foundation and industry support. He has been continuously funded by the NSF since receiving a CAREER award in 2013.
